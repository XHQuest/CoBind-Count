CoBind-Count data attribution
============================

Source data: CLEVR and CLEVR-CoGenT.
Authors: Justin Johnson, Bharath Hariharan, Laurens van der Maaten,
Li Fei-Fei, C. Lawrence Zitnick, Ross Girshick.
Work: CLEVR: A Diagnostic Dataset for Compositional Language and
Elementary Visual Reasoning, CVPR 2017.
Source: https://cs.stanford.edu/people/jcjohns/clevr/

The source project releases its data under Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Legal text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

CoBind-Count is a derivative data package with selected scenes,
train/development/test partitions, text prompts, joint-count targets,
diagnostic metadata, and counterfactual calibration targets.
Reference PNGs preserve the source image content.
The derived data is distributed under CC BY 4.0. Redistribution must
retain attribution and identify adaptations as required by that license.

CoBind-Count is an independent project, not an official release or
an endorsement by the CLEVR authors. This notice concerns dataset
content; third-party model weights and software have their own licenses.
